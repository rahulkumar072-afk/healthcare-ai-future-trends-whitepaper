# Future Trends and Innovation in Healthcare AI – Final Week 6 Project

This repository accompanies an evidence-based strategic whitepaper for the final YUVA/NSDC internship week.

## What is different about this project?
The report does not invent patient results. It combines future-trend analysis with published clinical studies, official regulatory sources, and real medical-dataset metadata. Every numerical case-study value is attributed to its source.

## Main topics
Generative/multimodal AI; agentic AI; AI-enabled medical devices; precision medicine and genomics; AI drug discovery; federated/privacy-preserving AI; synthetic health data; wearables; digital twins; robotics; ethics; cybersecurity; regulation; market readiness; stakeholder strategy; KPI framework; five-year roadmap.

## Real-world evidence
- TREWS sepsis study: 6,877 patients and reported mortality association.
- Lausanne AI sepsis system: 97,559 intervention stays and 25,851 control stays.
- Rentosertib AI-discovered drug: 71-patient phase 2a and published FVC results.
- AlphaFold DB: >200 million protein structure predictions.
- CheXpert: 224,316 chest radiographs from 65,240 patients.
- MIMIC-IV v3.0: 364,627 individuals, 546,028 hospitalizations and 94,458 ICU stays.
- FDA IDx-DR De Novo classification.
